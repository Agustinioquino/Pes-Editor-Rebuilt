## ⚽ What it's about

This version is for the ones who sit on the bench. The **Formation** tab now speaks the same language as the PES 2021 **Game Plan**: same options, same names, and each one explains what it does before you touch it.

None of this came from a guessed table. Every piece of data was tested **in the game**: change a value in the EDIT, open the Game Plan and take a screenshot. It took several rounds with Manchester United, Liverpool and Barcelona as guinea pigs (they all made it back safe and sound to their original tactics 😅).

---

## 🧠 Strategy: like in the game, but explained

Attack in **pink**, defense in **light blue**, just like in the game. Each option is a card: click it and it gets selected, and underneath it tells you what it does.

| Attack | Defense |
|---|---|
| Attacking Style · Build-up · Attacking Area · Positioning · Support Range | Defensive Style · Containment Area · Pressure · Defensive Line · Distance to the player on the ball |

### 🎯 Advanced Instructions: all 4, by name

The game allows **2 attacking and 2 defensive** per plan, and now so does the editor. You see them all together, click up to 2 per side, and each one shows whether it's *Attack 1* or *Attack 2*. Click a selected one and it's gone. And it won't let you repeat the same one, just like the game.

- **Attack:** Width, Attacking Full-backs, Flank Rotation, Tiki-taka, Deep-Lying Forward, Crosses into the box, False Full-backs and False Winger.
- **Defense:** Wing-back, Tight Marking, Deep defensive line, Box Crowding, High pressure and Free striker.

13 of the 14 are confirmed in the game. The missing one is **Free striker**: the game shows it as "No" if no striker is assigned, so in the editor it appears as *probable*.

### ⚡ Quick tactical options

The same ones as in the game, as cards with their description: *Long Ball Counter*, *Quick counter attacks*, *Possession Game*, *All-out Defence*, *All-out attack*, *Wide* and *Centre* (attacking or defensive). One click and several options change at once. The one that matches what your team has stays highlighted.

> [!NOTE]
> The game doesn't store the exact values of these options in the EDIT, so the editor uses similar ones.

### 🛡️ Marking settings

If some player got stuck marking a fixed opponent (and chases him all over the pitch when he shouldn't), the card tells you how many there are, and **"Back to automatic"** puts them back to default. Individual marking is still chosen in the game.

## 🎨 It wears the jersey of every graphics mode

Strategy adapts to each **Graphics** mode: Modern (light and dark), Classic with its raised borders, Retro with the PS2 blue, and PES 6 Edit with the golden selection.

## 🥅 Captain and set pieces

New card in **Formation**, same as "Player Options" in the Game Plan: captain, long free kick, short free kick, taker 2, left corner, right corner, penalties and the **3 attacking players** who go up into the box. Starters on top, substitutes below. The order of each slot is verified in the game, corners included.

<img alt="Captain and set pieces" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/pes-editor/main/docs/releases/v0.020/balon-parado.png" width="100%">

Can't be bothered to pick them one by one? **"Pick automatically"** puts a starter on each set piece based on their skills: free kick and power, curl, finishing, heading.

## 👕 Kit for the whole squad in one go

In **Squad**, select several players and click **"Kit…"**: sleeves, undershirt, socks, tights, untucked shirt, gloves, glove colour and boots for everyone at once. Only what you choose changes; everything else stays as it was.

<img alt="Kit for several players" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/pes-editor/main/docs/releases/v0.020/equipacion.png" width="100%">

## 🔒 Before saving, the editor checks the EDIT

Before writing or downloading your EDIT, the editor opens it again and checks that the counts and IDs of players, teams and squads add up. If it finds a problem that wasn't in your original file, it **doesn't save** and shows you the list. Better a warning than a broken EDIT.

## ✏️ Other changes

- **Compare with another EDIT** (File tab) now names each set-piece taker, each advanced instruction and the Marking settings, instead of showing numbers.
- New automated tests on GitHub: every change is tested by encrypting and decrypting PES 2020 and 2021 EDITs byte by byte, and by writing and reading every player and team field.
