## ⚽ What it's about

Want your 72-rated prospect to reach 80 without touching 25 numbers one by one? Now **you change the overall freehand**: drag a corner of the hexagon, or tap the rating in the squad and raise it with a button. The editor spreads the change across the abilities so the player **is still the same player**, just better (or worse, we don't judge 😅).

And since we update often, we also made sure that **updating costs you nothing**: before reloading, the editor asks whether to save, and your work stays saved in the browser cache.

---

## ⬡ Hexagon chart

In **Players → Abilities** there's a hexagon with six areas: **Attack, Technique, Passing, Speed, Physical and Defence** (goalkeepers have their own: Goalkeeper, Save, Clear, Reflexes, Reach and Physical). Each corner is the average of the abilities in that area.

- **Drag a corner** and all the abilities in that area go up or down by the same amount: the differences between them are kept.
- The **approximate rating** changes live while you drag, with the +/− it adds.
- A **dashed line** shows how it was when you opened the file, and each area says how much it changed.
- With the **keyboard** too: arrows for ±1, PgUp and PgDn for ±5.
- Every move is a single **Undo** step.

## ✎ The overall, from the squad

The editor's first screen is now for editing too: in the **Rtg.** column of the squad table, every rating has a pencil ✎. Tap it and a window opens **on top of the page**, without leaving the team.

<img alt="The squad table with a player's 75 rating marked with a pencil" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.043/plantel.png" width="100%">

| | What it does |
|---|---|
| **−5 −1 +1 +5** | Move the overall a little or a lot. |
| **Slider from 40 to 99** | Pick the overall directly; the hexagon shows live how it would end up. |
| **Hexagon** | To fine-tune a specific area without touching the others. |
| **Back to how it was** | Undoes everything in that window (it stays in Redo). |
| **Open full profile** | Takes you to the player's Abilities. |

For outfield players, all the playing abilities move equally (the goalkeeper ones aren't touched); for goalkeepers, the goalkeeper ones and jumping. If a player is in the game's database but not in your EDIT, the window offers to add them so you can edit them.

<img alt="The window for a player from the game who isn't in the EDIT yet, with the button to add them" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.043/parche.png" width="100%">

## ⬆️ The new-version notice, for real

- It checks **every 3 minutes** while the tab is in view (it used to be every 10) and as soon as you come back to it.
- It looks at the **latest change published on GitHub**: when one shows up, it checks every 30 seconds until the new page is ready.
- It shows **what changed**, with the title of the change.
- **Update now** requests every file again, so nothing from the previous version is left.

<img alt="The A new version of the editor is available notice with the title of the change and the Update now and Later buttons" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.043/aviso.png" width="100%">

## 🗂 Your work, safe in the cache

If you have unsaved changes and you tap **Update now** (or **Clear cache**), the editor asks you what to do:

- **🗂 Save to cache and reload**: your changes stay in this browser and come back on their own. The game's EDIT isn't touched.
- **💾 Save to the game and reload** (or **Save copy**, if you opened the file by hand).
- **Update without saving** or **Cancel**.

<img alt="The Update the editor box in PES 6 Edit with the Save to cache and Save to the game options" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.043/pregunta.png" width="100%">

When you come back, the editor opens **the same EDIT**, with your changes, the **Undo** history and on the **screen you were on**. If the game saved the EDIT in the meantime, it warns you before overwriting anything. What's saved in the cache lasts one day.

<img alt="The editor after reloading, with the notice Recovered 2 unsaved changes from the cache" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.043/recuperado.png" width="100%">
