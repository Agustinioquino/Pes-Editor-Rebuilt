## ⚽ What it's about

The time has come for **your club to have its own crest**. You upload an image and the editor takes care of the rest: the 11 files, the sizes, the ID and even the folder in Sider if it didn't exist.

Plus, the editor no longer sticks with yesterday's version: **it updates itself** and stores photos so they load in a flash. And if your Sider lives in *Program Files*, now you've got a way in 🔑.

---

## 🛡️ Upload your own crests

- In **Data and colours** there's a new section, **Crest**. You upload a PNG (or drag it in) and tap **Save crest to the game**.
- The editor builds the **11 files** the game uses (`f`, `f_l`, `f_ll`, `r`, `r_l`, `r_ll`, `r_b_l`, `r_b_ll`, `r_w`, `r_w_l`, `r_w_ll`), **each one scaled down to its size** and always with the team's **6-digit ID**, for example `e_002689_f.png`.
- Already have your files ready? Upload them all together: each one goes to its place and, if they carry a different ID, **it gets fixed automatically**.
- They're saved in `livecpk\…\common\render\symbol\flag`, in the folder that already held that team's crest. Whatever was there is kept as `.bak-edit21`, just in case.
- The new crest shows up right away in the editor. In the game, after you reopen it.

### 📂 If your Sider didn't have a crests folder

The editor creates `livecpk\PESEditor-Escudos` and adds it to `sider.ini` **ahead of the others**, so your crests take priority. The original `sider.ini` is saved as `sider.ini.bak-pes-editor`.

## 🔄 Always the latest version

- The editor **checks its files against GitHub every time it opens**: no more seeing the old version after an update.
- If something new is published **while the page is open**, **"A new version of the editor is available"** appears at the top with *Update now* or *Later*. If you have unsaved changes, it warns you first.
- Under the version number, in the top left, you can see **the date of the version** you're using.

<img alt="The new version bar in PES 6 Edit" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.041/version-nueva.png" width="100%">

## 📸 Photos and crests that load instantly

Photos and crests from the internet **are downloaded only once** and after that they come from the editor itself, in a flash and **offline too**.

### 🧹 Clear cache

In **🎨 Appearance** there's a **Cache** section (in all 4 graphics modes) with the version, how many photos are stored and the space used. If something looks off, **Clear cache** reloads everything with the latest version, without touching your settings or your EDIT.

<img alt="The Cache section in Appearance in PES 6 Edit" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.041/borrar-cache.png" width="100%">

## 🔑 Sider inside Program Files

If your game is in `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\…`, Chrome and Edge show **"Can't open this folder… it contains system files"** and won't let any page write there. Now the loading screen explains this from the start and offers:

- **📂 Open view-only**: kits, faces, crests and names from the game all work. Chrome asks whether you want to "upload" the files, but nothing gets uploaded.
- **To be able to save** to the game (kits, crests, module), move the game to another Steam library: right-click PES 2021 › Properties › Installed Files › Move install folder.
