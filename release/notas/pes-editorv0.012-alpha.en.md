### **⚽ PES Editor v0.012 alpha · Preseason**

**🎨 Interface:**
New font across the whole page: QuickExpress, with a sporty style. It was repaired (Chrome wouldn't accept it), Spanish letters were added (á, ñ, ü, ç, ¿, ¡…) and it was narrowed so it fits in buttons and tabs. You can change it from **🎨 Appearance.**

**🖥️ Graphics button, next to Appearance:**

> Modes: Modern, Classic (the old-school desktop editor), Retro (PS2 menus) and PES 6 Edit.

> Quality:

 Low removes animations and effects for slow PCs; High draws the 3D at a higher resolution and maxes out the effects. It shows how many frames per second the page is running at.

> PES 6 Edit style: steel blue, silver bars, the selection highlighted in orange and gold, and menu sounds as you move around and tap. They're synthesized, not ripped from the game, and you can turn them off in Graphics.

> "Save to the game" is now level with the center of the logo.

**🎵 Player:**

> The songs you add come with their cover art, title and artist (MP3, FLAC, M4A). If they have no cover art, one is made up with colors.

> It no longer falls apart with long names.

**> ▶ YouTube**: paste the link to a playlist or a video and they're added to the player. Videos show in a small window, because YouTube doesn't allow hiding them.

**📖 README**
"Connect Sider folder" step better explained.
Notice for when names or ratings don't show up.
