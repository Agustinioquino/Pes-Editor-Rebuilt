## ⚽ What it's about

v0.020 was for the manager. This one's for the kit man: **no new tactics, it's all about the looks**. PES 6 Edit mode gets nostalgic, the squad shows you more at a glance and the loading screen walks you through connecting everything.

If you ever sat staring at the PES 6 Edit menu with the background music on instead of playing, this version is for you 📺.

---

## 📺 PES 6 Edit, like on the PS2 in the living room

- **Stadium background** with a slight blur and an **old TV (CRT)** effect: scanlines, a touch of color bleed and darker corners.
- **Golden squad table**, soft so it reads well, with the starters highlighted.
- **Square corners** on everything, like the menus of that era.
- **The logo breathes**: it slowly grows and shrinks, with a second outlined layer that follows along.
- **The Graphics and Appearance buttons** are easy to read, silver with dark lettering.

### 📜 The text ticker at the bottom

The PES 6 classic: a ribbon that scrolls slowly, seamlessly, in white PES lettering. It runs through the editor's news, future ideas and trivia about the series. And if you hover over a tab or a button, it stops and explains what it does.

<img alt="The whole interface in PES 6 Edit" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/pes-editor/main/docs/releases/v0.030/pes6-plantel.png" width="100%">

## 📋 The squad, at a glance

- **Real flags** next to the nationality. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland each with their own, as it should be. Without internet, you get the national team's crest.
- **Bigger photos**, with a **border and background by position**: goalkeeper in gold, defender in blue, midfielder in green and forward in red.
- **Photos or Kits**: a toggle to see the faces or just the team's shirt.
- **The column is called "Player name"**, in the export too.
- **Averages explained**: at the bottom it says that overall, age and height are squad averages, and that the value is the sum of everyone.
- **The real stadium in the header**: at the top you see the real name and, in brackets, the one your EDIT has selected. For example, *Anfield (Rose Park Stadium)*.

## 📸 Photos from two places

For players who don't have a miniface in the game, you now choose where the photos come from:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| ✂️ **Cut-out** | Only background-free photos from TheSportsDB. The ones that aren't there stay with the shirt. |
| 🧩 **Blend** | First the TheSportsDB cut-out and, if there isn't one, the real photo from Wikipedia. |
| 🚫 **No online photos** | Only the game's minifaces. |

You choose once while loading and can change it whenever you want in **Formation**, with the Photos view. Next to it you'll see how long each source takes and how many photos it brought in.

## 🚪 A loading screen that walks with you

After opening the EDIT, the loading screen **asks you to connect the Sider folder**. If it was already connected and the browser lost the permission, it offers to grant it again. It only goes in once the faces, crests and fonts have finished loading, so you don't see the page half-built.

<img alt="The loading screen asks you to connect Sider and choose the photos" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/pes-editor/main/docs/releases/v0.030/carga.png" width="100%">

The button for loading names is now called **"Connect Player.bin"**, which is what it does.

## 🖥️ The whole screen

- On PC the editor takes up **the full width**, top bar included.
- The **header fits on a single row**.
- **Appearance** has icons and previews of each palette, grouped by theme.
- Each player's actions (captain, move to another team…) are in the **⋯** button.
- In **File**, on a wide screen, everything arranges itself in two columns.

<img alt="The Appearance panel with icons" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/pes-editor/main/docs/releases/v0.030/apariencia.png" width="100%">

## 🎵 Quieter music

- When nothing is playing, the player shrinks to **a circle**.
- **15 seconds** after a YouTube video starts, **only the cover art** remains. Tap it and the video comes back.

## 🔧 Fixes

- In PES 6 Edit and Classic, with **Windows in dark mode**, the duplicate shirt number and the checkboxes showed up black. Not anymore.
- In Retro, the number on the active tab is easy to read.
