## ⚽ What it's about

The editor did its preseason in England and came back **speaking English**. On top of that, Formation got friendlier (move several players at once, a longer bench and the Transfermarkt value), the teams panel got wider and each graphics mode has its own animations.

And the YouTube video, which kept playing dumb and wouldn't leave, finally got the message 📺👋.

---

## 🇬🇧 The editor in English

- **Language picker with a little flag**, next to Reload: 🇪🇸 Español or 🇬🇧 English. The page reloads in the language you chose and remembers it for next time.
- **Everything you see gets translated**: tabs, buttons, tips, notices, What's new, the PES 6 text ticker, countries, positions and playing styles. That's about **2,600 phrases**.
- **From the loading screen too**, top right next to Music, so you don't even need to go in to change it.
- Spanish is still the original version: if you pick Español, the editor stays exactly as it always was.

<img alt="The language picker open in Spanish and in English" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.040/ingles.png" width="100%">

## 🧠 A friendlier Formation

- **Move several players together.** Tap players while holding **Ctrl**, or use **"Pick several"**: they get marked and when you drag one they all move, keeping the distance between them. "Pick the 10 outfield players" moves the whole team except the goalkeeper, and **Undo** reverts it all in one go.
- **Longer substitutes bench.** When you don't have a player selected, the substitutes list stretches out and you see many more without scrolling.

<img alt="Two strikers selected at once on the Formation pitch, with the long substitutes bench beside it" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.040/formacion.png" width="100%">

### 💶 Transfermarkt value

The Formation header shows the **Transfermarkt value** next to the **estimated value**. You fill it in by tapping it, the **↗** opens the team's search on Transfermarkt, and underneath it tells you **how far off** it is from the estimate.

And it dresses up to match the mode: in **Retro** it's a PS2 menu box with pixel borders, and in **PES 6 Edit** a silver plate with the number in gold.

<img alt="The Formation header in PES 6 Edit, Retro, Modern and Classic" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.040/transfermarkt.png" width="100%">

## 🗂️ Wider teams panel

- **More room** for long names.
- **Sort** now sits next to All, Edited and Unsaved.
- **League or country picker**, with how many clubs each one has. For example, *England (45)*.
- New sort **by Country**.

<img alt="The teams panel filtered by England" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.040/equipos.png" width="100%">

## 🛡️ Crests from Wikipedia

If TheSportsDB doesn't have a club's crest, it's now looked up on **the club's Wikipedia page**. Clubs that had been left with the empty little square get searched again automatically.

## ✨ Each mode with its own personality

- **Animated tabs**: a shine in Modern, an orange stripe in Classic, a blinking little arrow in Retro and a red trace that loops around in PES 6.
- **TV static in PES 6 Edit**: when you switch tab, team or section there's a burst of static, like when you changed the channel to play.
- **The logo echo** now goes from the inside out.

## 🎵 The video really goes away

- After **15 seconds** the YouTube frame **disappears completely** and you're left with the player's small cover art with a ▶. Tap it and it comes back.
- New **✕** on the video: it removes it for all the upcoming tracks, and the music keeps playing.

<img alt="The YouTube video with its ✕, and the editor with the video already hidden" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.040/musica.png" width="100%">

## 🔧 Fixes

- **The YouTube video sometimes didn't hide** after 15 seconds, because it relied on signals from YouTube that didn't always arrive. Now it's measured with the video's actual playback time.
- **When it hid, the frame still took up space** with the cover art inside. Now it goes away entirely.
- **Clubs without a crest forever**: if TheSportsDB didn't find them once, they were never searched again. Now they're retried, with Wikipedia as a fallback.
- **The links to the editor** on the project page point to the new address.
