## ⚽ What is your game loading? Now you can see it

Until today, there was only one way to know which mods Sider was loading, in what order, or whether a folder in `sider.ini` no longer existed: **open sider.ini in Notepad** and read it line by line. 📜

Not anymore. Next to WEPES there's a new tab, **Sider**, that reads your `sider.ini` and shows it to you for what it is: a list of mods.

- **Content folders (`cpk.root`)**, each with a label for what it has inside (🛡 crests, 🪪 player photos, 🙂 3D faces, 👕 kits, 🏟 stadiums, ⚽ balls, 📦 datapack…) and which editor tool uses it. The editor looks inside the folder to find out; if it can only guess from the name, the label says so.
- **Lua modules (`lua.module`)**: kit server, faces, chants, scoreboards, balls, stadiums, `edit21_db`… with a line saying what each one does.
- **`content` folders**, linked to the module that uses them (the kit server with its teams in `map.txt`, the copy of the game database…).
- And at the very top: the Sider version, the game executable and the `livecpk` folders that aren't in `sider.ini` yet.

<img alt="The Lua modules section in Retro mode: kserv, faceserver, chants, ballserver and StadiumServer, each with its switch, its description and its arrows" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/sider-modulos.png" width="100%">

## 🎛 Turn on, turn off, reorder and add, without touching the text

Every folder and every module has its own **switch**: turning it off comments out the line in `sider.ini` (it doesn't delete it, so you can turn it back on whenever you want). The **arrows** move it up and down, because order matters in Sider: if two folders have the same file, **the one higher up wins**. The **cross** removes the line. And anything that's in `livecpk` or `modules` but not in `sider.ini` can be added with a button.

Sider's **settings** (livecpk, Lua modules, close Sider on exit, pick either side, black bars, camera, overlay, log detail…) are there as switches and lists, each with a line explaining what it's for. And if `livecpk` or the modules are turned off, if a `cpk.root` folder doesn't exist or if a module is missing from `modules`, it **warns you at the top** instead of letting you find out mid-match.

<img alt="Sider's settings in Classic mode, as switches with their explanations, and the log detail as a list" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/sider-ajustes.png" width="100%">

**Nothing is written until you click "Save sider.ini".** Until then you see "Unsaved changes" and you can undo them. The first time, a copy is kept in `sider.ini.bak-pes-editor`. Only the lines you touched are rewritten: comments and everything else stay exactly as they were, and the file is saved **with the same encoding it had** (UTF-8, UTF-16 or Notepad's with accents), so Sider keeps reading it like always. If you're one of those who prefer the text, it's also there in full to view and edit.

## 🚦 Loading now checks sider.ini (and waits for the crests)

The loading screen learned two things:

1. **It waits for the crests and photos** of what's on screen, so the editor shows up complete and not in bits and pieces. The first time, if it takes a while, "Go in now" appears after 6 seconds.
2. After "Sider ready", **it reads sider.ini** and tells you how many content folders and modules it has and of what type. If something's wrong (a folder that doesn't exist, a missing module, `livecpk` turned off), a ⚠ step appears with the details and, when it finishes, a notice with a button straight to the Sider tab.

## 📂 Any Sider and any EDIT

Connecting Sider **no longer requires one particular setup's kit server**. Any of these works:

- the **Sider folder** of any setup, even if it doesn't have a `sider.ini` yet (like **Football Life**, which keeps `livecpk` and `modules` right in the game folder);
- the **game folder**, or one further up (Steam, steamapps): the editor searches a few levels down, and doesn't mix up Steam's `common` with a cpk's `common`.

Faces and crests come from the folders listed in `sider.ini` and from the rest of `livecpk`. And if you picked a folder that won't work, the notice **tells you what it looks like** (a content folder, `livecpk`, `content`, the game without Sider, an empty folder or one with your own stuff) and which one you need to pick:

<img alt="The notice when picking the wrong folder, in Classic mode: Sider isn't in that folder or its subfolders, inside there are 7 folders like Videos, Work, Music…" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/carpeta-equivocada.png" width="100%">

The **EDIT** is also found in the non-Steam version (`<season>\save`), in any save subfolder and in Football Life-style folders.

## 🙈 The editor says there's no sider.ini, but it's there? Pick it by hand

We found this out thanks to a report from this very week: **Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave don't let pages see `.ini`, `.exe` or `.dll` files**, not even when listing the folder. As far as the browser is concerned, `sider.ini` simply doesn't exist. 🤯

So now, if the editor can't find `sider.ini` when you connect the folder, the Sider tab shows you **what it sees inside the folder** and gives you the **"📄 Pick sider.ini by hand"** button: you pick it once with the file picker and the editor remembers it, for reading and for saving. In the **Windows app** you don't need this, because it doesn't go through the browser.

<img alt="The Sider tab when the browser hides sider.ini: it explains why, lists what it sees in the folder and offers the Pick sider.ini by hand button" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/sider-ini-a-mano.png" width="100%">

> 🧪 **In testing.** We tested it thoroughly with a test Sider and by simulating what the browser does, but not yet on a PC with a real game setup. If the button isn't enough with yours, [let us know](https://github.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/issues/new/choose).

## 🎵 Music by link, no keys or accounts

In the player, **"🔗 Add by link"** opens a single box. Paste the link to a **YouTube video or playlist**, or to a **Spotify song, album or playlist**, and it's added to your list. No API keys, no connecting accounts. If you type a name instead of a link, the YouTube or Spotify search opens in another tab so you can copy the link you like.

<img alt="The player in PES 6 Edit with the Add by link box: typing champions anthem offers Search on YouTube and Search on Spotify" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/musica-link.png" width="100%">

In **PES Editor Rebuilt for Windows** it's even more direct: you type the name and **the app searches YouTube right there**; tap the result and you're done.

<img alt="The player in Retro mode inside the app: the search champions anthem shows four YouTube results to add with one tap" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.045/musica-app.png" width="100%">

One detail: full Spotify needs your Premium account signed in in the little Spotify window; otherwise, a 30-second preview plays.
