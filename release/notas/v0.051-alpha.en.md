## 🐛 The bug

You made a change to the EDIT, clicked the Windows app's **X** and… nothing. The window just sat there, not closing and not saying why.

What was going on? With **unsaved changes**, the browser shows its "Leave site?" warning. The app held back the close just like the browser does, but that warning never showed up. Result: a window that wouldn't go away.

## ✅ The fix

Now, if you click the X with unsaved changes, the **🚪 Exit the editor** box appears with your options:

- **🗂 Save to cache and exit**: your changes are kept in the app and come back on their own the next time you open that EDIT (for one day), Undo included.
- **💾 Save to game and exit**: they're written to the EDIT, just like with the Save button.
- **Exit without saving** or **Cancel**.

You choose and the app closes. With no pending changes, it closes right away like always.

## 🔧 While we were at it

- **F5 or Ctrl+R** in the app show the same box, but for **reloading**.
- **Changing the language** and **Update without saving** no longer get stuck on the warning: changing the language saves your changes to the cache and they come back on their own.

## ⬇️ Do I need to download anything?

**No.** The fix is in the editor, and the app loads it from the internet on its own: the next time you open it (or when "A new version of the editor is available" shows up) you've got it. The new installer is the same program as always, in case you don't have it yet.
