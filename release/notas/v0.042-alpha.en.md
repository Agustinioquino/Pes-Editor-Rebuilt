## ⚽ What it's about

Has your striker been scoring every single weekend? Now **the rating finds out**. Form no longer comes only from the last 6 matches: it looks at **the whole season**, who's tearing it up in the scoring charts, **who's injured** and how each player did in previous seasons.

And the best part: it's not a black box anymore. Every player has an **ⓘ** button that tells you **where every point comes from** 🔍.

---

## 🔎 Search finds your team

Your EDIT says **Como 1907**, but on ESPN it's just **Como**. It used to miss it; now the editor tries the name without the year, without abbreviations (FC, Club, Calcio…) and with the most important word, and uses the **team's country in your EDIT** so it doesn't get mixed up with Comoros or the women's team.

## 🗓 The whole season, top scorers and injuries

- With the **ESPN** source, the editor analyzes every match of the season **in the background**: goals, assists, minutes and each player's rating.
- At the top you'll see the season's **⚽ top scorers** and **🅰 assists**, the previous season with its trend, and how many players are **🤕 injured**.
- If you don't want it, untick **"Analyze the whole season"** and it goes back to using only the recent matches.

## 📊 Form, with everything that happens on the pitch

Form now adds up six parts, each one in rating points:

| | What it looks at |
|---|---|
| 📈 **Recent matches** | Each match's rating against what's expected of him (the latest match and minutes played weigh more). |
| 🗓 **Season** | His average rating over the whole season and what share of the minutes he plays. |
| ⚽ **Goals and assists** | Per 90 minutes, against what's normal for his position. |
| 🏟 **Team** | Points per match and whether the team improved on last year. |
| 📚 **History** | His previous seasons: a short hot streak doesn't turn him into a superstar. |
| 🤕 **Injuries** | Injured −1 (−1.5 if out for over two months), doubtful −0.5, a regular who doesn't show up −1. |

Then **age** acts as a multiplier (young players rise faster, veterans drop faster) and the usual **caps** apply: up to +4 for young players and down to −3 (−4 for veterans).
