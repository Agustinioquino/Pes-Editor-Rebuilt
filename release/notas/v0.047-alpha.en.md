> ⚠️ **Preview version.** Everything in this version is new and has had little testing with real games: it will very likely have bugs. Before loading or replacing kits, make a copy of your `content\kit-server` folder. If something goes wrong, [tell us](https://github.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/issues/new/choose) what happened and with which kit.

The Kit studio is now called **Kit Studio Pro** and grows into two parts. And the most requested thing: **you downloaded a kit and want to see it in the game without touching a single file by hand**. Now you just drag it in and that's it.

## 📦 Drag in the .zip and pick the slot

Drop the kit's .zip (or its loose textures: `.ftex`, `.dds`, `.png`) on the **Uniforms** tab. A box shows **what it includes**: kit, normal map, shine, back, chest and shorts numbers, name letters. You choose which slot it goes in: **starter (p1), substitute (p2), third, fourth or goalkeeper**.

- If the slot **already has a kit**, you decide: **replace it** (the old one is kept in `_borrados`) or **add it as a new kit**.
- Whatever is **missing** (normal map, shine, name letters) is **generated generically** with the studio's fabric and font.
- Everything is converted to the game's format (**FTEX**) and the `config.txt` (with the colors read from the texture if it doesn't come with one) and the `order.ini` / `gk_order.ini` are written automatically.

## 🔒 The kits that live in the .cpk files

If the team **has no folder in the kit-server**, its kits are encrypted inside the game's `.cpk` files and can't be opened from the editor. Kit Studio Pro now says so clearly, and **still lets you load a kit for it through Sider**: the team's folder and its line in `map.txt` are created.

<img alt="The card of a team with no folder in the kit-server: the .cpk notice and the area for dragging in a kit" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.047/equipo-cpk.png" width="100%">

## 🗂 Uniforms and Editor

- **Uniforms**: all of the team's kits with their thumbnail textures, in the game's order. From there you can **edit**, **move up in the order**, **duplicate** (⧉), **copy a player kit as a goalkeeper kit** (🧤) and **delete** (🗑, the folder stays in `_borrados`).
- **Editor**: one kit in wide screen, with **Enlarge**, **full screen** and **Game view**, which imitates the game's framing.

<img alt="The Kit Studio Pro Editor with the kit strip, the 3D viewer and the camera and clothing panels" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Agustinioquino/Pes-Editor-Rebuilt/main/docs/releases/v0.047/editor.png" width="100%">

## ✥ Move the name and numbers on the model

In the 3D viewer, the **name**, the **back number**, the **chest number** and the **shorts number** can be dragged straight onto the shirt; the **mouse wheel** over them changes their size. The values go into `config.txt` (`NameY`, `BackNumberY`, `ChestNumberX/Y`, `ShortsNumberX/Y` and the sizes), respecting each kit's range. The positions come from an approximate calculation: **they're not calibrated against the game yet**, so there may be differences.

## 🧵 And also

- Textures load at their **full resolution** (up to 4096), no longer shrunk to 1024.
- The font generator has **"Shorts number in a different colour"**.
- A texture uploaded to a kit **no longer disappears** when you switch kits and come back.
