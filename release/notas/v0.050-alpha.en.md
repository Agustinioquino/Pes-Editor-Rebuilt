## 📣 Attention, attention: the day has come

Do you play **Football Life 2026**? Then this version is yours. **PES Editor Rebuilt now supports Football Life 2026**: it reads its full database, finds its EDIT, understands its Sider and shows the ratings the way the game shows them. No tricks, no weird steps, no opening the game first.

We tested it with a real Football Life 2026 install (version 2.2.0) and the result speaks for itself:

> ### ⚽ 29,413 players · 749 teams · in half a second
> Read straight from the game files, **classics included**: Čech, Beckenbauer and the whole gang of legends that Football Life brings.

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Player and team database | You had to open the game with the `edit21_db` module | **Read on its own from the game files** |
| Ratings | Up to 109, like in PES 2021 | **Capped at 104, like in Football Life** |
| The EDIT (`2026\save`) | Hunt for it by hand | **Finds it on its own** (and there's a 📂 Find EDIT button) |
| The Sider tab | Just another Sider | **⚽ Football Life 2026 chip** |

### 🚀 Get started in three steps

1. Click **Connect Sider folder**.
2. Pick the **game folder** (the one with `FL_2026.exe`) or one higher up.
3. Done: the names, teams and ratings come from Football Life. Your EDIT is in `Documentos\KONAMI\eFootball PES 2021 SEASON UPDATE\2026\save` and the editor finds it on its own.

And everything keeps working the same with **PES 2021**, with or without mods: the editor checks which game you have and adapts.

## 📚 The game database, without opening the game

Connect the game folder (for example `B:\footballlife`, or the one above it) and the editor **reads the database straight from its files**: the `data_s2526*.cpk` files in `download`, in the same order the game loads them (taken from `spFileList.bin`), so the newest update always wins. The Sider folders go on top, just like in the game.

- **29,413 players and 749 teams** in half a second, classics included (Čech, Beckenbauer and company).
- No need to open the game with the `edit21_db` module first. If the module already left its copy, the newer of the two is used.
- The `edit21_db.lua` module works the same in Football Life: Sider hands it the database just like in PES 2021.
- The Sider tab shows the **⚽ Football Life 2026** chip when the Sider is the one from `SiderAddons`.

## 🎯 Ratings the way each game shows them

In PES 2021 a rating can go up to **109**; in Football Life the cap is **104**. The editor checks which game it is (by the EDIT folder, which in Football Life is `…\2026\save`, or by the `.exe` of the game connected with Sider) and shows the ratings with the right cap, in the quick rating and the hexagon too.

## 📂 Find EDIT

Next to **Reload** there's a new button, **📂 Find EDIT**. It shows every EDIT it found (with its date and size) so you can switch between them, searches Documents again, and lets you pick the EDIT folder or open the file by hand. Perfect if you have PES 2021 and Football Life on the same PC.
