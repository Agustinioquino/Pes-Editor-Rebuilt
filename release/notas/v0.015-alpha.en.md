## ⚽ PES Editor v0.015 alpha · YouTube fine-tuned

### 🎵 Player
- **The volume stays put when you switch YouTube tracks.** Before, the new video could start muted: the browser muted the second player because you hadn't interacted with it. Now all videos play in a single player, at the volume you picked. Between two videos the handoff is: fade down, switch, fade up.
- **Smaller video, off to the side.** It's 200 × 200 (the minimum YouTube allows) and sits next to the player, without covering the team list. With the panel open it takes the place of the cover art.
- **On phones, no video on top of the page.** The YouTube video only shows with the player open. Since YouTube doesn't let its audio keep playing with the video hidden, closing the panel pauses that track and it plays again when you open it. Your own songs always play.
